# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, professional, and harassment-free
environment for everyone participating in this project.

## Expected Behavior

- Communicate respectfully and constructively.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people.
- Welcome questions and different experience levels.
- Protect confidential, personal, and proprietary information.
- Accept responsibility, correct mistakes, and learn from feedback.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, deliberate disclosure
of private information, and other conduct that makes participation unsafe or
unprofessional are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may
temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior violates
these expectations.

Report conduct concerns privately to the project owner through an appropriate
private GitHub contact method. Do not include sensitive details in a public
issue.
